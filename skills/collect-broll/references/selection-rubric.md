# B-roll selection rubric

Score candidates only after checking the source page, metadata, thumbnails, or frames. Do not rank from titles alone.

## Required gates

Reject a candidate when any required gate fails:

- the requested person, place, action, or event is not actually visible
- the useful shot is only a static photo, thumbnail, or commentator reaction
- the clip is an obvious duplicate of an already selected item
- the download cannot be completed without bypassing DRM or access controls
- an obvious stock-preview watermark covers the useful image
- the clip contains excluded graphic material
- the file cannot be decoded

## Scoring

Score each surviving candidate from 0 to 3 on each dimension:

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Semantic match | Weak or inferred | Subject appears briefly | Clear match | Exact requested action and mood |
| Editability | Mostly narration/graphics | Short usable moment | Several usable shots | Sustained clean B-roll |
| Visual expression | Flat or obscured | Limited movement | Clear expression/action | Strong gesture, motion, or emotional beat |
| Technical quality | Unusable | Archival exception | 720p or clean SD archive | Clean 1080p+ |
| Source quality | Untraceable mirror | Secondary compilation | Reputable publisher | Original/official source |
| Provenance | Missing | Partial | Original URL and uploader | Date/event/license context also known |

Apply penalties:

- minus 3 for prominent channel graphics, captions, or logos covering the subject
- minus 3 for near-duplicate framing or the same event moment
- minus 2 for misleading editing or heavy commentary
- minus 2 for an unnecessarily long download with little usable footage

Select the highest-scoring varied set, not four nearly identical clips.

## Personal defaults for this workspace

- Exclude Getty Images unless the user explicitly asks for it.
- Exclude visible stock-preview watermarks.
- Prefer source-native footage over compilations.
- Prefer 1080p; allow lower resolution for scarce historical footage and disclose it.
- Avoid graphic gore and visible corpses unless explicitly requested.
- Preserve source IDs in filenames.
- When people are requested, favor sustained close/medium shots, readable expression, and body language.
- When action is requested, favor the action itself over aftermath or commentary.

## Visual QA

Generate early, middle, and late frames for every downloaded file. Inspect the resulting contact sheet and replace any clip whose actual image fails the brief. A successful download is not a successful selection.
