# Question patterns

Use these patterns as diagnostic structures, not fill-in-the-blank prose. Keep the host's natural voice.

## Continuation patterns

### Claim → mechanism

“您提到 X。它听起来像是一种结果，但中间真正发生作用的机制是什么？”

Use when the guest states a correlation, cycle, or historical observation without explaining how it reaches the individual.

### Claim → boundary

“如果 X 成立，它在什么条件下会失效？Y 会不会构成反例？”

Use to prevent a broad thesis from becoming universal.

### Same appearance → different cause

“A 和 B 表面上都表现为 X，但一个可能来自安全感，另一个可能来自失望。我们怎样区分？”

Use when the episode compares generations, countries, or similar behaviors with different mechanisms.

### Ideal → cost

“我们都认同 X 的价值，但谁承担它的成本？普通人需要什么条件才有资格这样生活？”

Use for freedom, creativity, relaxation, spiritual pursuit, or technological progress.

### Then → now

“这个判断是在某个历史阶段形成的。今天环境改变后，它仍然成立，还是需要换一种版本？”

Use for long-termism, work ethics, success narratives, education, or technology forecasts.

### Social structure → personal experience

“如果 X 不只是性格问题，而是结构留下的结果，一个人还可以在哪些地方重新获得能动性？”

Use to return macro discussion to the listener without moralizing.

## Core-transformation patterns

### Method → life choice

Turn a method from the guest's work into a decision problem: provisional belief, experimentation, updating, uncertainty, or evidence thresholds.

Good transformation: ask what would count as evidence to persist or change.

Weak transformation: say that life “is exactly like” the scientific concept.

### Epistemic limit → human value

If a work describes what logic, language, or AI cannot do, ask what remains for human judgment and how that judgment is disciplined.

Always include the danger of prejudice, impulse, or power; do not romanticize intuition.

### Institutional idea → meaning infrastructure

If a work discusses scientific communities, schools, or organizations, ask whether meaning and long-term commitment also require communities and institutions.

### Historical system → secular belief

If a work explains religion, industrialization, or progress as a system of certainty, ask whether success, work, or growth narratives perform a similar function today.

### Incompleteness → self-understanding

Ask whether a complete and internally consistent identity is possible or desirable. Keep this philosophical unless the guest explicitly makes a psychological claim.

## TIANYU2FM style check

A strong question usually contains four moves:

1. accurately state the guest's idea;
2. expose a tension or apparent contradiction;
3. identify why it matters now;
4. land on a question the guest has room to answer unexpectedly.

Do not force all four moves when a shorter question is stronger.
